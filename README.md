# Kidney Health & Dialysis Educational Booklet & Impact Survey 📑🩺

## Overview
This repository hosts a public health education initiative featuring a simplified 8-page educational booklet written in Easy Arabic. The booklet aims to introduce the general public to basic kidney health, causes of kidney failure, preventive guidelines, and an overview of dialysis.

To assess the practical value and knowledge gained by readers, an interactive Pre/Post-Reading Evaluation Survey was distributed alongside the booklet across Telegram channels.

---

## 📖 Booklet Contents (8 Pages)
The booklet provides clear, concise, and accessible information organized into 8 pages:

1. Cover Page: Title and initiative overview.
2. Introduction & Pre-Survey: Overview and direct access to the pre-reading assessment.
3. Kidney Functions: How healthy kidneys work and their primary roles in the body.
4. Kidney Failure Definition & Types: Overview of Acute vs. Chronic Kidney Disease.
5. Common Causes & Risk Factors: Diabetes, Hypertension, and Painkiller Misuse.
6. Signs & Symptoms: Common warning indicators to look out for.
7. Diagnostic Methods: Basic laboratory tests (Blood, Urine) and Imaging (Ultrasound).
8. Dialysis Overview & Post-Survey: Basic comparison between Hemodialysis and Peritoneal Dialysis, followed by the Post-Reading Impact Survey.

---

## 📊 Engagement & Impact
- Reach: Reached 1,500+ total views/impressions across participating Telegram channels.
- Evaluation: Evaluated reader comprehension and health literacy enhancement through a Pre/Post reading survey (15 completed participant responses).
